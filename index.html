PhD candidate in economics

Tommaso Tulkens

I am a PhD candidate at the Erasmus School of Economics in Rotterdam and a fellow of the Tinbergen Institute, supervised by Olivier Marie and Anne Gielen.

I work on applied microeconometrics and policy evaluation. Most of my research asks how the places people live in, and the rules that govern access to them, shape work, welfare and crime. I like research that keeps an eye on multidisciplinarity — especially the dialogue between history and economics.
